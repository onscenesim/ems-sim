# Standalone low-acuity calls

Nine fictional encounters enter the ordinary category pools and the instructor
catalog. Search **Low-acuity call —** in instructor mode. These are independent
cases, separate from the existing ambiguous frequent-caller pairs. Their titles
and case keys are private; dispatch and first impression do not reveal acuity.

| Pool | Call |
| --- | --- |
| Behavioral | Unconscious dispatch becomes a coffee/ride request, then police trespass pressure and situational threats |
| Respiratory | Familiar CHF caller, real chronic illness at baseline, reported vomiting and deliberate shaking |
| Medical | Immobile patient's leaking catheter bag and external tubing kink; son at home |
| Medical | Laryngectomy patient with no identified new complaint, communication barriers and a blind caregiver |
| Toxicology | First hangover, fixed request for hospital; adolescent or nonelderly adult |
| Behavioral | Bus-stop weakness report becomes an interruptible assault/attempted robbery |
| Trauma | Catastrophic dispatch becomes a no-injury parking-lot collision |
| Cardiac | Ward inpatient calls 911 with symptoms later admitted to be untrue |
| Cardiac | Urgent-care provider insists a normal ECG is a STEMI and alarms an asymptomatic patient |

Outside Black Cloud, the roller forces a stable trajectory and no deterioration
clock, suppresses random disease bundles/special circumstances, replaces medical
curveballs with equipment friction and excludes catastrophes. Not investigating
does not reveal a surprise lethal diagnosis. Scene safety still matters, and
harmful provider actions retain their consequences. Black Cloud retains normal
decline and disaster mechanics. Explicit instructor overrides remain available.

Partner reactions follow the named or custom character: sincere overcalling,
anger on behalf of the system, personal resentment, community concern, or
skeptical thoroughness. An early case-specific reaction can override passivity;
it never permits invented findings, automatic transport or unrolled procedures.
Debrief evaluates the recorded decisions, uncertainty, communication, crew bias
and proportionate disposition without inventing harm from an omitted workup.

Provider-initiated refusal/non-conveyance requires applicable service policy,
capacity/safety assessment and medical direction where required. Suicidal or
homicidal statements still need assessment. Neither disability nor a normal
saturation or ECG independently establishes safety. Transport on an informed
patient's request remains an acceptable course.

The urgent-care referral produces a normal synthetic 12-lead paper in the
existing ECG viewer when handed over or reviewed, once per call. This is a
facility document, available even to a BLS crew without a monitor; it does not
grant acquisition or interpretation privileges. Subsequent EMS acquisitions
still use the usual equipment, rolls and current physiology. The original
referral paper does not change if Black Cloud later changes the patient.

Run `npm run preview:low-acuity` and open
<http://127.0.0.1:3146/low-acuity-preview> for real model replies through Session
in isolated temporary storage. Choose any case, partner and difficulty; start,
arrive, investigate, wait without treatment, or enter a custom action.

Clinical portrayal references (care remains governed by local protocols):

- [National Tracheostomy Safety Project: laryngectomy algorithm](https://tracheostomy.org.uk/e-learning-files/NTSP_01_006-The_Patient_With_a_Laryngectomy_lo_pack/Laryngectomy_Algorithm.pdf): the stoma is the airway; assess the tube/inner cannula when relevant.
- [University Hospitals Sussex: catheter advice](https://www.uhsussex.nhs.uk/resources/catheters-advice-for-patients-discharged-from-the-emergency-department-with-a-new-urethral-catheter/): actual obstruction needs attention; this authored case is specifically an external bag/tubing issue without retention.
- [NIH: suicide](https://health.nih.gov/health-topics-a-z/suicide): suicidal statements warrant attention even when the encounter has an apparent situational motive.
